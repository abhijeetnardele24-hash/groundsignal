# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, mobility, disability, or
technology choice.

Be constructive, respect privacy, obtain consent before collecting another
person's data, and avoid claims that dismiss or generalize another person's
lived mobility experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and
publication of private telemetry are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Project maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.
