# Three-minute judge demo

## 0:00–0:25 — The hook

“GroundSignal turns the motion you physically feel into a transparent surface
report. The sidewalk becomes the dataset, so the useful part happens outside,
not on a screen.”

Show the dashboard, the privacy status, and the local field ledger.

## 0:25–0:55 — Personal calibration

Show mobility mode and phone placement. Explain why calibration is personal:
gait, wheels, device hardware, and placement change the signal. Open two saved
surface types and start one short sample.

## 0:55–1:20 — Pocket Mode

Show the dark recording surface on the phone. Point out that it intentionally
has no map, feed, or task list. The screen is meant to disappear into a pocket.

## 1:20–2:05 — Open AI analysis

Stop the walk and show the report. Explain that overlapping motion windows are
converted into interpretable features and sent to TabPFN; raw telemetry stays
local. Show the provider name, probability-derived confidence, route signature,
and abstention behavior.

## 2:05–2:35 — Human judgment

Correct one uncertain segment and discard another. Add reviewed signals to the
personal calibration. Emphasize that the model does not silently turn its own
prediction into truth.

## 2:35–3:00 — Evidence and limits

Export CSV and JSON. Show the architecture diagram and baseline-versus-TabPFN
metrics. End with: “This is a field observation—not a safety guarantee or a
universal accessibility assessment.”
